Using NakliOS

NakliOS (naklios.dev) is free to use. It runs in your browser and keeps your data on your device. You are responsible for the data you put in it and for keeping your own backups.

Connected accounts

When you connect an outside account, such as Google, you allow the app you are using to act on your behalf within the permissions you approved, for example to read or send mail. You can withdraw that at any time in Settings → AI → Reset Google sign-ins, or in your Google account. Your use of Google's services stays under Google's own terms. How NakliOS handles that data is described in the privacy policy.

AI features

AI features run on a model you choose: one in your browser, or a service whose address and key you entered. AI output can be wrong. Check a drafted mail or a proposed event before you send or save it; NakliOS asks you to confirm before an app sends mail or changes your calendar.
