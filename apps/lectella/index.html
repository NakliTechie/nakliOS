Lectella

    A notebook for each thing you are researching

    Add your sources: paste text, or open a PDF, Markdown or text file. Write notes beside them. Ask a question and the answer comes from your sources, with numbered citations you can click to read the passage.

    Everything is kept as plain files: on your NakliOS Folder or Crate when one is connected, or in this browser's private storage. Nothing is uploaded. Chat uses the model you pick in NakliOS; search works without any model.
