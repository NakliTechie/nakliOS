Licences and source

Reel plays video files on your device. It ships the third-party software below, each under its own licence. The licence texts are files in this app; the links open them.

Reel

Reel's own code (the page, app/, engine/reel-engine.mjs, engine/gpu-renderer.mjs, engine/libav-rung.mjs) is by NakliTechie. Its licence is not yet published; it will be stated here and in the repository.

Corresponding source for the LGPL parts

You may replace the LGPL libraries: they are separate files. For the FFmpeg build in vendor/libav/, the corresponding source is:

  • libav.js, tag v6.10.9.0, commit c80e885c3461f7bb7ea565c9631b34243ae0dbf1, including its FFmpeg patches in patches/ffmpeg/: github.com/Yahweasel/libav.js. Reel adds no patch of its own.
  • FFmpeg 9.0: ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.tar.xz, SHA-256 7f607a00dd0d28a729d5a4811205812eef01cf6ef6155025febb6f36a9062d52.
  • The build configuration: mkconfig-reel.json (the parts list given to libav.js configs/mkconfig.js as variant reel), built with make dist/libav-6.10.9.0-reel.mjs dist/libav-6.10.9.0-reel.wasm.mjs in the container emscripten/emsdk@sha256:6bd7aef93408b10bf7b36642cb7c91119b302f720b9ec2d20439f73cf07fe894.
  • The FFmpeg WebAssembly inside @mediabunny/ac3 and @mediabunny/dts: github.com/Vanilagy/mediabunny, tag v1.66.0, packages/ac3 and packages/dts.
  • FriBidi inside JASSUB 1.8.8: github.com/ThaUnknown/jassub at 1.8.8 and its build scripts.
