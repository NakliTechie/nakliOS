NakliOS privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. NakliOS is made by Chirag Patnaik (NakliTechie).

NakliOS (naklios.dev) is a desktop that runs in your browser. Its apps keep your data on your device: in your browser's storage, or in a folder you pick. NakliOS has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it runs no server that stores your files, mail or calendar.

Data from your Google account

Some NakliOS apps (Mail and Calendar) can connect to your Google account when you ask them to. When they do:

  • What they ask for. Only the permissions shown on Google's consent screen and in NakliOS's own prompt before it, for example "read, send, label and delete your Gmail" or "see and edit events in your Google calendars". Each app asks separately.
  • Where the data goes. Your browser talks to Google directly. Mail, events and contacts read from Google are shown on your screen and may be cached on your device, in your browser's storage or the folder you chose for NakliOS. They are not sent to NakliTechie or to any server NakliTechie runs.
  • Sign-in tokens. Google gives NakliOS a token that lasts one hour. NakliOS keeps it in the browser's memory only, never on disk, and never stores your Google password. NakliOS keeps a local log of which app signed in and when, without the token.
  • AI features. If you choose to use an AI feature on your mail or calendar (for example "draft a reply"), the text you select goes to the AI model you configured in NakliOS: one running in your browser, or an AI service whose address and key you entered yourself. NakliTechie does not provide or see that service. Data from Google is never used to train AI models.
  • No selling or sharing. Data from Google is used only to show you your own mail and calendar and to do what you ask. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone.

NakliOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • In NakliOS, open Settings → AI and press Reset Google sign-ins. This revokes the tokens NakliOS holds and makes every app ask again.
  • You can also remove NakliOS at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Cached mail and events live in your browser's site data for naklios.dev, or in the folder you picked. Clearing that site data, or deleting the folder, removes them.

Other data

naklios.dev is served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for security and operation. NakliOS itself sets no tracking cookies.
